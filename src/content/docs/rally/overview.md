Rally is the first RallyX product: a platform for booking badminton courts in
Vietnam.

## The problem

Booking a court today runs on group chats and phone calls. Someone asks the
group who is free, someone else calls a venue, the venue checks a paper book,
prices get quoted from memory, and the whole exchange repeats next week. Nobody
in that loop has a reliable picture of what is actually available.

The cost lands on both sides:

- **Players** cannot see which courts are free before committing to a time.
- **Venues** lose slots to no-shows and spend staff time on the phone.

## What Rally does

Rally puts real court availability in one place and lets a booking complete
without a conversation:

- See which courts are free, when, and at what price.
- Book a slot and have it confirmed.
- Let venues manage their own availability instead of a paper book.

## Who it is for

- **Players** organising a session, alone or for a group.
- **Venues** that want their courts filled and their schedule to be accurate.

## How it is built

Rally follows the [RallyX missions](/missions/). In practice that means the
booking flow is short enough to finish on a phone on the way to work, prices are
what they say they are, and the feature list stays small on purpose.
