RallyX is a research lab for Vietnamese innovations.

We are not an agency and not a feature factory. We pick a problem that people in
Vietnam actually hit, study it properly, and build the smallest real product that
solves it. If the research says the product should not exist, we do not build it.

## What "lab" means here

Three things, in order:

1. **Research first.** Understand the problem in its local context — how the
   people involved already work around it today, and what they would have to give
   up to switch.
2. **Build the smallest thing that works.** One product, one job, done well.
   Scope is a decision, not an accident.
3. **Ship it and stay with it.** A product we no longer maintain is a product we
   should not have shipped.

## Why Vietnam specifically

Software built elsewhere and translated rarely fits. Payment habits, phone
habits, how people organise a group, what they trust and what they are wary of —
all of it is local. RallyX builds from that starting point rather than adapting to
it afterwards.

## What we are working on

[Rally](/rally/overview/) is the first product: a booking platform for badminton
courts in Vietnam.

## How we decide

Every product is held to [five missions](/missions/). They are the bar, not a
slogan — if a change cannot be defended against them, it does not ship.
